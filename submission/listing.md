# Loopy! — CrazyGames listing draft (not submitted)

**Title:** Loopy!

**Short description (1–2 sentences):**
Draw a loop around fish of the same color to pop them. Go big, chain combos, trigger Frenzy and see how long you can keep the pond from filling up.

**Description:**
Loopy! is a relaxing but addictive loop-and-pop game. Fish swim around the pond in five colors. Draw a loop around fish of ONE color to pop them. The more fish in one loop, the bigger the score, so go for huge loops. Mix colors and your net tangles.

Look out for the special fish: golden fish are wild, rainbow fish make any loop valid, puffer fish blow up everything near them, and the King is worth a fortune. Fill the Frenzy meter for a burst of double points, and don't let the pond overflow.

Earn coins to unlock hats, ropes and pond looks. Collect every fish, sheep and toy ball in your album, finish daily missions and complete achievements. Take a break with the fishing minigame.

**Controls (use the text the portal asks for):**
- Mouse: hold the left button and draw a loop around the fish, then release. Closing the loop pops them.
- Touch: draw with your finger.
- P: pause. Enter or Space on the main menu: start.

**Orientation:** landscape and portrait. **Languages:** English. **Rating target:** PEGI 12 (no violence, no gambling mechanics, no real-money purchases, no external links).

**Tags to consider:** casual, arcade, puzzle, relaxing, one-touch, fish, collecting.
